Selling on eBay in the US

Fees

These come from eBay's fee page for a seller without a store. Open it to confirm, since eBay changes rates and shows different pages in different cases: eBay selling fees.

CategoryFinal value fee
Most categories, including Pottery and Glass and Antiques13.6 percent up to 7,500 dollars per item, then 2.35 percent on the rest
Jewelry and Watches15 percent up to 5,000 dollars per item, then 9 percent on the rest
  • Per-order fee: 0.30 dollars for orders of 10 dollars or less, 0.40 dollars above that.
  • The first 250 listings each month are free, then 0.35 dollars each.
  • The fee applies to the total sale amount, which can include shipping and tax. Check the fee page for how it applies to you.

Example, not a quote: a 100 dollar glass item costs about 14 dollars in fees, so you keep about 86 dollars before shipping and your cost.

New seller limits

New accounts usually have a limit on how many items and how many dollars you can sell each month. Reports put a typical start around 10 items and 500 dollars, but it varies by account. Good sales and on-time shipping let you ask for a higher limit after a few weeks. Check your own limit in Seller Hub.

Writing a listing

  • Title: up to 80 characters. Lead with the words a buyer would search: maker, item type, color, pattern, size.
  • Choose the exact category and fill in every item specific.
  • Use all your photo slots.
  • Be literal about condition. List chips, cracks, scratches, cloudiness and repairs. Give measurements and weight.
  • Use the words antique, vintage and reproduction correctly. Call something antique only if you have evidence it is 100 years or older.
  • For jewelry, say what the stamp says and whether you tested the metal. Say "untested" if you did not.

Returns and the buyer guarantee

Even if you choose no returns, eBay's Money Back Guarantee can force a refund when an item is not as described or arrives damaged, and you may pay return shipping. Accurate descriptions and careful packing are your protection.

Authenticity Guarantee

eBay authenticates some jewelry and watches above a price threshold before they reach the buyer. Thresholds and categories change, so check eBay's current Authenticity Guarantee page when you list higher-value pieces.

Handling time

Ship within one to three business days and upload tracking right away. Short handling time helps buyers trust you.
