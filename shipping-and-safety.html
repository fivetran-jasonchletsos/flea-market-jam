Shipping Glass and Staying Safe

Packing glass and china

  • Wrap each piece alone in bubble wrap.
  • Use a box with at least two inches of cushioning on every side, and suspend the item so it does not touch the walls.
  • For valuable pieces, use a box inside a second box.
  • Label the box fragile, but do not count on the label.
  • Take a photo of the packed box and keep your receipt and tracking.
  • Insure higher-value pieces.

This is common reseller practice, not a carrier or platform rule.

When it arrives broken

On eBay, an item that arrives damaged is returned at the seller's expense with a refund. Ask the buyer for photos of the damage and the packaging, and file with the carrier if you insured it. Good packing and insurance are cheaper than refunds.

What to ship, and what to sell local

Ship small, light, valuable pieces. Sell large, heavy, fragile or low-value pieces locally and skip the shipping. Furniture is almost always a local sale.

Scams

See the list on the Marketplace lesson. The rule that covers all of them: only trust money you can see in your own account, keep talking inside the app, and never share a code a stranger asks for.

Your address

Do not post your address. Meet buyers in public. When you publish photos on this site, check that mail, family photos and street views are out of the frame.
