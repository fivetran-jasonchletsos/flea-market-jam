Selling on Etsy

Check before you rely on this. Etsy's own help pages could not be read when this lesson was written, so the numbers below come from seller guides and blogs. Read Etsy's fee and policy pages in a browser before you open a shop: fees, prohibited items, creativity standards.

Fees as reported

  • Listing fee: 0.20 dollars per listing.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5 percent of the order total, including shipping.
  • Payment processing in the US: 3 percent plus 0.25 dollars.
  • Offsite Ads: 15 percent of a sale that comes through the ad for shops under 10,000 dollars in yearly sales, and 12 percent above that, with a cap per order. You pay it only when an ad leads to a sale.

The vintage rule

Etsy treats an item as vintage when it is at least 20 years old at the time you list it. Etsy has no separate antique category. Use the "when made" field, and put the era in the title and description. Say how you dated it, such as a maker's mark, a pattern name or a patent number. Use "circa" when you are unsure.

Etsy is for handmade, vintage and craft supplies. Do not list new mass-produced goods as vintage or handmade.

Listing limits

  • Title up to 140 characters.
  • 13 tags, each up to 20 characters. Use phrases a buyer would search.
  • Up to 20 photos and one short video. Larger photos zoom better.
  • Fill in every attribute that applies, such as color and material.

What not to list

  • Ivory, bone, tortoiseshell and similar animal products, even if old. Etsy aims to be free of these.
  • Radioactive material appears on Etsy's prohibited list. Uranium glass is a gray area, so check the policy text or avoid it as a beginner.
  • Do not claim glass is food-safe.

Shipping and protection

You set a processing time and a return policy for each listing. Buy tracked shipping. Etsy's Purchase Protection for Sellers covers qualifying orders up to a set amount when you meet its tracking and delivery rules. Read the current rules on Etsy before you rely on them.

Payouts

Etsy pays out to your bank account on a schedule you choose. New shops often wait a few days for funds to become available.

Why listings are made by hand

Etsy has a developer program that can create listings, but it needs an approved app and a separate setup. The scanner here gives you drafts to paste instead.
