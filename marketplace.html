Selling on Facebook Marketplace

Check before you rely on this. Facebook's help pages could not be read when this lesson was written. The fees came from a seller guide, so confirm them in the app before you list.

Fees as reported

  • Local sale where the buyer pays you directly: no selling fee.
  • Shipped sale paid through Facebook checkout: 10 percent of the item price plus shipping and tax, with a minimum fee of 0.80 dollars.
  • A chargeback can cost a fee plus the item value. Keep proof of delivery and tracking.

Shipping and payout

  • For shipped sales you buy your own label and enter tracking.
  • Payout starts about 5 days after delivery is confirmed, or 15 days after you mark it shipped, whichever comes first, plus a few days to reach the bank. You need a valid tracking number and a connected bank account.

Best uses

Marketplace works best for furniture, heavy items and common glass, sold locally with cash and no shipping. Leave room in the price for haggling.

Meeting a buyer safely

  • Meet in a public place, such as the lobby of a bank or police station. Bring a friend and tell someone where you are going.
  • For furniture, put it by the door or in the driveway. Do not let strangers into your home.
  • Keep messages inside the app.
  • Take cash, or confirm the money in your own payment app before you hand the item over.

Scams to know

  • Overpayment. A buyer says they paid too much and asks you to send back the difference. The original payment is fake.
  • Fake payment screenshots. Only trust money you can see in your own account.
  • Code requests. A buyer says they sent you a code and asks you to read it back. They are trying to take over an account using your phone number. Never share a code.
  • Payment links and deposits. Gift cards, Zelle deposits or "friends and family" payments to hold an item. End the deal.
  • Cashier's checks for too much. The check bounces days later.
  • Shipper pickups. A buyer who says their shipper will collect it and asks for your details or a fee first.

Meta lists its own safety advice here: Marketplace safety.

What you cannot list

Weapons and ammunition, animals, drugs, tobacco, currency and recalled products are off limits. Antiques and jewelry are generally allowed. Do not list ivory, tortoiseshell or antique firearms. Read Facebook's commerce policies.

Why listings are made by hand

Facebook has no listing tool for individual sellers. Use the scanner drafts and paste them into the app.
