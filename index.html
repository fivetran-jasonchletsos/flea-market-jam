Flea Market Jam

A place to learn to sell cobalt glass, jewelry and antiques on eBay, Etsy and Facebook Marketplace. Take photos of an item with the scanner to get a suggested identification, a price range and draft listings. Then use the lessons to list it well.

ScannerPhotograph an item. Get an identification, a price range, flags and draft listings. Needs a passcode. InventoryEverything scanned and published, with photos, prices and drafts. Download it as a spreadsheet. LessonsTen short lessons on photos, pricing, each platform, identifying items and the rules.

Where to start

  1. Read Start Here and Photos and Pricing.
  2. Scan three items you already own.
  3. Check each price against eBay sold listings using the links in the results.
  4. Read the lesson for the platform you will use first.
Prices from the scanner are AI estimates, not live sales data. Fees and rules change, so each lesson links to the official page to check.
