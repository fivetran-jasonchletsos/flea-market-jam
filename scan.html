Flea Market Jam Scanner Inventory Lessons

Scanner

Tip: in Chrome, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen, so this opens with one tap.

Take 1 to 4 photos of an item. The scanner suggests what it is, a price range, and draft listings for eBay, Etsy and Marketplace. The price is an AI estimate, not live sales data. Always check it against sold listings using the links in the results.

Photos you publish are public. Anyone can see them on the Inventory page. Before you shoot, move mail, family photos, windows showing your street, and anything with your address out of the frame. Location data is stripped from every photo automatically.
